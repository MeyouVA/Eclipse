Drawing

The drawing tools

  • Brush: a stroke is a filled shape with a contour of a few points. Pressure changes its width.
  • Pencil: a centre line with one thickness, and a choice of ends and corners.
  • Line, Rectangle, Ellipse, Polyline: shapes made of pencil lines. Shift keeps a line to 15 degrees and makes squares and circles. A polyline takes a click for each point (drag to bend it), and Enter to end.
  • Eraser: cuts what it passes over out of shapes, paint and lines.
  • Stamp: a click puts a copy of the stamp there: a star to begin with, or artwork of your own (select it, then Drawing > Create Stamp from Selection).
  • Text: click, type, Enter. Click on words again to change them.
  • Stroke and Close Gap: lines with no thickness. They are not seen, but paint stops at them. View > Show > Show Strokes makes them visible.

Changing what is drawn

  • Select: click or draw round artwork, then drag it, pull a corner to scale, or the handle above to turn. The arrow keys nudge it. Tool Properties has buttons to flip, rotate, flatten, smooth, group and convert, and boxes to move and scale by numbers.
  • Cutter: draw round part of the artwork to cut it loose and select it.
  • Contour Editor: move the points and handles of a line or a brush shape. Ctrl+click on the line adds a point.
  • Perspective: pull each corner of the box round the selection on its own, to put the artwork in perspective.
  • Smooth Editor: rub over a line or the edge of a brush shape to smooth it there.
  • Pencil Editor: press on a point of a pencil line and pull away from it or towards it to make the line thicker or thinner there.
  • Reposition All Drawings: moves, scales or turns every drawing of the layer at once.

Art layers

Every drawing has a Line Art and a Colour Art sheet (and Overlay and Underlay, if turned on in Preferences). Tools work on the sheet chosen with the buttons beside the Camera view. Drawing > Art Layer > Create Colour Art from Line Art copies the lines as invisible strokes, so the colour can be painted on its own sheet.

Guides

Windows > Guides adds straight guides to snap to, and guides to draw along: a ruler, grids, and 1-, 2- and 3-point perspective. A Symmetry guide mirrors what you draw across its axis. View > Alignment Guides adds, locks and clears the straight ones; the Select tool drags them.

Cleaning up

Drawing > Clean Up removes specks and short stray lines, closes small gaps, and removes the artwork inside or outside the selection. Drawing > Optimize flattens, smooths and reduces points.
