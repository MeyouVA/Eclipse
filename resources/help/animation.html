Moving layers, the camera and depth

Keyframes

  • Choose the Transform tool and drag a layer: it is moved on this frame, and a keyframe is made. Between two keyframes the layer glides from one pose to the other.
  • Corners scale, the handle turns, the small circle is the pivot it turns about.
  • Animation > Tools has Translate, Rotate, Scale, Skew and Maintain Size (nearer or further, looking the same size): the Transform tool doing one thing only, wherever you take hold of the layer.
  • The first time a layer is moved on a frame after the first, frame 1 gets a keyframe too, of the layer as it was (a preference).
  • A stop-motion keyframe holds its pose until the next keyframe instead of gliding.
  • Animation > Set Ease makes a move start or stop gently. The Function view shows the values as curves to drag; the keyframe on the current frame has two ease handles on its curve.
  • The Breakdown Assistant (Windows menu) puts a keyframe on the current frame part of the way between the keyframes either side of it.
  • Animation > Animate > Animate Off: the Transform tool then moves the layer in all its keyframes alike.
  • The + after a layer's name in the Timeline opens a row for each value.

The camera

Insert > Camera adds a camera layer. Moved, scaled and turned like any layer, it changes what the frame sees. A scene can have several; Scene > Camera chooses one.

Depth

Scene > Scene Settings > Projection can be Orthographic: depth then only puts layers in front of and behind one another.

Every layer has a depth (Z). Nearer layers look bigger and slide by faster when the camera moves. The Top, Side and Perspective views show the layers in space. With the Transform tool, hold Alt to drag a layer nearer or further.

Morphing

Animation > Morphing > Create Morphing makes the frames between two key drawings by itself, changing one into the other.

The Morphing tool puts down hints - a point on the first drawing and where it goes on the second. In its properties, morphing layers are named groups of shapes: Ctrl+click a shape to put it in the group chosen, and the shapes of a group only change into shapes of the same group.

Pose Controllers

The Pose Controllers view (Windows menu) keeps named poses of several layers - a hand open, a hand closed - and a slider that blends between them, or a pad with the poses as dots to drag a point among. The slider and the point can be keyframed: their keyframes have a row of their own in the Timeline.

3D models

A layer made by File > Import > 3D Models shows the model turned as the layer is turned in space. Animation > 3D has Set 3D Animation Timing (a run of model files played from a frame, so many frames each), Snapshot 3D (the model as it is seen on this frame kept as a flat picture on a layer of its own) and Update 3D Model Position (the model files read again and the models put back in the middle of the frame).
