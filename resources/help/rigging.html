Cut-out characters and deformers

A cut-out character

  1. Draw each part (arm, forearm, hand...) on a layer of its own.
  2. Attach each part to the part it hangs from, by dragging its layer onto that one in the Timeline or joining them in the Node View.
  3. Put each pivot at its joint with the Transform tool.
  4. Pose with the Transform tool, keyframe by keyframe. The Inverse Kinematics tool pulls a hand and lets the arm follow.

Deformers

  • The Rigging tool has three modes in Tool Properties. Bone: click from joint to joint to give a drawing layer a chain of bones that bends it. Curve: click along the artwork, and it bends along the curve. Envelope: click round the artwork, and it is pulled about by its outline.
  • Pose a deformer with the Transform tool; each pose is a keyframe.
  • Rigging > Deformation resets or removes a deformer, or turns what it shows into plain drawings.

Templates

Select the layers of a finished character and choose Create Template from Selection in the Library view: it can then be dragged into any scene.
