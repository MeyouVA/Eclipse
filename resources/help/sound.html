Sound and lip-sync

  • File > Import > Sound puts a sound on a sound layer; its wave shows along the Timeline. Drag it to move it in time.
  • Play > Enable Sound Scrubbing: a frame of the sound is heard whenever you change frame by hand.
  • Animation > Lip-Sync > Auto Lip-Sync Detection gives each frame of the sound one of eight mouth shapes (A to G, and X at rest). It goes by loudness, not by words, so treat it as a first pass.
  • The Lip-Sync view shows the sound frame by frame, to correct a frame from a chart of the mouths.
  • Map Lip-Sync puts the drawing you choose for each shape on the frames of a drawing layer.
