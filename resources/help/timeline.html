Timeline and Xsheet

Layers

  • The + button adds a layer: Drawing, Peg, Camera, Colour-Card, Group, Bone, Quadmap or Sound.
  • Each layer has switches: enabled, solo, locked, onion skin, and a track colour.
  • The Timeline menu can tag layers, and its View Modes show only the tagged layers, or only the ones selected.
  • Drag a layer onto another to attach it: it then moves with that layer. A peg is a layer with no drawings, used to move others.

Cells and exposure

  • A drawing is held until the next one. + and - lengthen or shorten its exposure.
  • Draw on an empty frame further along and the drawing before it is held up to there. (Edit > Preferences > Extend Exposure of Previous Drawing turns that off: the frames between then stay empty.)
  • Animation > Cell: Extend Exposure, Clear Exposure, Fill Empty Cells, Insert Blank Cell, Create Cycle, and more.
  • Drag over cells to select a block. Cut, Copy, Paste and Delete act on it; dragging moves it (Ctrl copies).
  • Drawing > Duplicate Drawings gives a frame a copy of its own to change. The Drawing Substitution view picks which drawing a frame shows.

The ruler

  • Drag along it to go through the frames. The red brackets are the start and end of the scene.
  • The blue bar is the onion skin range.
  • Scene markers are coloured bands with a name, made from the Timeline menu.

Xsheet

The Xsheet shows the same scene as a table: a column for each layer, a row for each frame.

Double-click a cell and type the name or number of a drawing to show it on that frame; Enter goes on to the frame below. A name no drawing has makes a new, empty one. Drawing > Rename Drawing gives a drawing a name.
