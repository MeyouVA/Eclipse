Keys and customising

  • Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts lists every command and lets you give it a key of your own.
  • Right-click a toolbar and choose Customize to choose its buttons.
  • Edit > Preferences has the settings of the program itself.
  • Windows > Workspace keeps arrangements of the views under names; Restore Default Workspace puts them back as they came.

Keys worth knowing

Space + dragslide the view
, and .previous and next frame
+ and -longer and shorter exposure
Lswitch between Line Art and Colour Art
Kshow strokes
Shift+Llight table
Alt+2 to Alt+6Translate, Rotate, Scale, Skew, Maintain Size
Ctrl+Drename the drawing
Ctrl+Enterrender and play
Shift+Dremove dirt
Arrow keysnudge the selection (Shift: ten times as far)
Shift while drawinga straight brush or pencil line; a square or a circle; a line every 15 degrees
Alt while drawinga rectangle or ellipse from its middle
Shift while movinglevel or upright only
Escabandon what is being dragged
F1this help

The keys shown beside the commands in the menus are always the ones in use.
