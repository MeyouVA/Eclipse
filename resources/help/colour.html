Colour and paint

Palettes

  • A scene has one or more palettes; each colour used in a drawing is a colour of a palette. Change a colour in the palette and everything drawn with it changes.
  • Double-click a colour to change it. Right-click for gradients, moving it, and Protect Colour.
  • Palettes can be exported to a file and imported into another scene.
  • Right-click a palette and choose Link to File to share it between scenes: each scene linked to the file takes its colours when it is opened and writes its own there when it is saved.

Painting

  • Paint: click inside a closed area to fill it, or on a line or paint to recolour it. Drag round several areas to paint them all.
  • Paint Unpainted fills only empty areas; Repaint only changes what is painted; Unpaint takes paint away (and makes a line invisible).
  • If an area will not fill, its lines have a gap: use Close Gap, or Drawing > Clean Up > Close Gaps.
  • Ink: drag over lines to give them the colour in hand; paint is left alone.
  • Dropper: takes the colour of what is clicked.
  • Drawing > Paint repaints or unpaints the selection, or everything outside it, in this drawing or in all drawings.
  • A protected colour is left alone by the paint tools while Respect Protected Colour is ticked.

Gradients

A colour can be a gradient, in a line or round. The Edit Gradient tool places it in a painted area by dragging across the area.

Seeing colours

View > Show > Highlight Selected Colour shows everything that is not the colour in hand faintly. Backlight shows all artwork as a dark shape, to check for unpainted spots.
